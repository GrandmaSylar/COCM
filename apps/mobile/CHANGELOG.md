# Changelog: CoC Mataheko mobile app

Versions follow `apps/mobile/app.json` (`version`, and the Android `versionCode`).

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-01)

- Added the Services page, as on the web: a list with the latest service and a monthly timeline, a detail view (overview, setup, finances, people, children), and the service setup form with an order-of-service PDF preview and share. It opens offline from the last saved copy.
- Added converting a visitor to a member, from a service's People section and from a Convert button on the Visitors list.
- Upgraded to Expo SDK 58.
